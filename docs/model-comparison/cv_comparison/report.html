Paired differences vs baseline

Each row is a model, with one lane per horizon. MAE/MAPE are relative to hgb_mean; pinball/width are relative to hgb_quantile_q7. Large markers: pooled mean difference over all test rows, with a 95% moving-block bootstrap interval (14-day blocks over daily differences; conditional on the fitted models, it ignores training randomness). Small dots: mean difference in each of the 8 CV folds (one dot per fold; hover shows the fold). Folds are not independent, so no t-interval across folds is shown. The dashed zero line means no difference; lower is better for every panel (losses and width). A model whose interval excludes zero and whose dots are all on one side is consistently different from the baseline; dots on both sides of zero mean the sign depends on the period.

Coverage vs interval width

Each point is one model/horizon, using the mean coverage and mean interval width across the 8 folds. The vertical dashed line is the nominal 90% coverage. Points close to the line are well calibrated; lower is sharper.

Absolute metrics by horizon

Horizontal bars show the mean across the 8 CV folds, grouped by horizon (1h, 12h, 24h), without error bars. The table above pools all rows across folds, so its values can differ slightly, especially for the nonlinear pinball-d2 metrics.
