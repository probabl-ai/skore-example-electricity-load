Reliability / calibration diagrams

Pareto plots: average MAPE and pinball loss vs fit + predict time

Paired differences vs baseline

Bars show each model's mean paired difference from the baseline, grouped by horizon. MAE/MAPE are relative to hgb_mean; pinball/width are relative to hgb_quantile_q7. Error bars are 95% paired t-intervals across the 8 folds; pairing removes the common seasonal/fold difficulty. The dashed zero line means no difference. For MAE, MAPE and width, negative is better; for d2 pinball, positive is better. With only 8 correlated folds, treat these intervals as descriptive.

Coverage vs interval width

Each point is one model/horizon, using the mean coverage and mean interval width across the 8 folds. The vertical dashed line is the nominal 90% coverage. Points close to the line are well calibrated; lower is sharper.
