Selected feature groups on all lagged-feature models

The three feature groups that helped in round 1 (load_diffs, load_same_hour, temp_diffs) are added to the default features of every lagged-feature model. Each model is compared with its earlier run with default features, with the full 8-fold walk-forward CV and safe-lead forecast weather. All average differences are significant, and the new features are better in 8/8 folds except TabICL (6/8, no gain at 24h). Kumo-Tabular remains the best model (655 vs 757 MW for the best HGB) but the gap shrinks.

  • load_diffs: differences between consecutive hourly load lags, and lag_h minus lag_24/48/168.
  • load_same_hour: load 1 and 7 days before the same clock hour (as extra lags), and lag_h minus them.
  • temp_diffs: national mean temperature change over 24h, 168h and h, and anomalies against its 24h / 168h means.

Cells: default → new (difference); * = the 95% moving-block bootstrap interval of the paired difference (14-day blocks over daily differences of the pooled test rows) excludes 0. It covers test-period variability only, not training randomness. Pinball is the mean over the 5% and 95% levels.

Caveats

  • The runs with default features were not repeated: they come from other commits, hosts and package versions (see their READMEs in results/model_comparison/). The hgb_mean result with these groups (786.4 MW) is the same on the dev machine and cpu-1, so HGB results do not depend on the host.
  • TabICL and Kumo-Tabular ran with torch 2.11.0 where their default-feature runs used 2.15.0, and are stochastic ensembles: differences of a few MW may be noise. One run per row, no seed repetition.
  • The recalibrated and conformal HGBs are still worse than plain hgb_quantile on MAE.
