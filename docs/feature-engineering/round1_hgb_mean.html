Feature groups for lagged-feature models (hgb_mean)

Adding differences of load lags, same-clock-hour load comparisons and temperature changes lowers the average MAE of hgb_mean from 872 to 773 MW (-99 MW, MAPE 1.78% to 1.59%), better in all 8 CV folds. load_diffs helps at 1h and 12h, temp_diffs at 24h. National temperature aggregates, degree days, extra rolling statistics, calendar distances and school holidays do nothing on their own.

Each row adds the listed group(s) to the default features (--feature_groups of cross_validate_models.py). Differences are paired against the baseline run; * = the 95% block-bootstrap interval excludes 0. The interval is a 95% moving-block bootstrap (14-day blocks over daily differences of the pooled test rows); it is conditional on the fitted models and ignores training randomness.

Caveats

  • All 7 groups together (773 MW) beat the best 3-group combination (786 MW) although the 4 other groups are neutral alone; not yet explained (interaction or run-to-run noise).
  • Only hgb_mean was run; hgb_quantile, TabICL and Kumo-Tabular are untested.
